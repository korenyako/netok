## Netok 0.6.0 — Portuguese, split in two

This release is all about language: Portuguese now comes in two flavors, plus a round of translation polish across every locale.

### What's new
- **Portuguese is now offered as two variants — Brazilian (pt-BR) and European (pt-PT).** Same app, the right words for where you are. Thanks to community feedback for the nudge that kicked this off.

### Improvements
- **"Start minimized"** is now translated in every language (it was showing in English for 12 of them).
- **Download / Upload** are now properly localized in French and Spanish.
- Clearer device-scan wording — and a grammar fix so the device count reads correctly in Polish.

### Fixes
- Removed leftover English words (like "Latency") that were slipping into Russian, Ukrainian, Korean, Italian, Polish, and Turkish.

### Under the hood
- Added a check that fails the build if any of the 15 languages falls out of sync with English — translations can't silently drift anymore.
- Cleaned out an old unused codebase and tidied the system-tray menu.

**Windows note:** Netok isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first launch — click *More info → Run anyway*.
